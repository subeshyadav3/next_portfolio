## Step 1: Choose the Right Blogging Platform

The platform you choose determines how much control, flexibility, and earning potential your blog has. Here are the two best options for Nepali bloggers in 2025.

### Option A: Blogger.com — Best for Absolute Beginners (Free)

Blogger is a free blogging platform owned by Google. You sign up with your Gmail account and get a free subdomain like `yourblog.blogspot.com`.

**Pros:**
- Completely free — no hosting or domain costs
- Backed by Google, so it is reliable and secure
- Easy to connect with Google AdSense
- No technical knowledge required to get started

**Cons:**
- Limited design customization compared to WordPress
- Your blog lives on Google's servers — you do not fully own it
- The `blogspot.com` subdomain looks less professional
- Fewer plugins and growth tools available

> **Best for:** Students and beginners who want to test blogging before investing money.

---

### Option B: WordPress.org — Best for Serious Bloggers (Recommended)

WordPress.org is the world's most popular blogging and website platform, powering over 43% of all websites globally. It is free software, but you need to pay for hosting and a domain name.

**Pros:**
- Full control over your website design and functionality
- Thousands of free and premium themes and plugins
- Better SEO capabilities with plugins like Yoast SEO or Rank Math
- More professional appearance, which helps with AdSense and brand deals
- You fully own your content and website

**Cons:**
- Requires purchasing hosting (approximately NPR 800–2,000/year) and a domain (approximately NPR 1,200–1,500/year for `.com`)
- Slightly more setup involved than Blogger

> **2025 Hosting Recommendation for Nepal:** Look into **Subisu**, **WebSurfer Nepal**, or **Hostinger** (international, affordable). Many offer Nepal-based payment options including eSewa and Khalti.

## Step 2: Set Up Your Blog

### Setting Up on Blogger (Free)

1. Go to [blogger.com](https://www.blogger.com) and sign in with your Google account.
2. Click **Create New Blog**.
3. Enter a blog title that reflects your niche (e.g., "Nepal Travel Tips" or "Tech Reviews Nepal").
4. Choose a URL — pick something short, memorable, and relevant.
5. Select a template or skip and customize later.

**After setup, do these immediately:**

- Go to **Settings → Basic** and add a clear blog description.
- Go to **Settings → Search Preferences → Meta Tags** and enable the meta description field.
- Add this robots.txt under **Search Preferences → Custom Robots.txt**:

```txt
User-agent: Mediapartners-Google
Disallow:

User-agent: *
Disallow: /search
Allow: /

Sitemap: [https://YOURBLOGNAME.blogspot.com/sitemap.xml](https://YOURBLOGNAME.blogspot.com/sitemap.xml)
```

6. Go to **Settings → Language and Formatting** and keep your language set to English for AdSense review.
7. Finally, submit your blog to **Google Search Console** at [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console). Add your blog as a property, verify it, and submit your sitemap. This tells Google your blog exists and speeds up indexing — a blog that is not indexed cannot be approved for AdSense.

### Setting Up on WordPress.org (Recommended)

1. Buy a domain name (for example from GoDaddy, Namecheap, or Subisu) and a hosting plan (Hostinger, WebSurfer, or Subisu).
2. Install WordPress through your hosting control panel — most hosts do this with one click.
3. Choose a clean, fast theme and install the **Rank Math** or **Yoast SEO** plugin.
4. In **Settings → General**, set your site title and tagline, and under **Permalinks** choose the "Post name" option so your URLs look professional.
5. Install an SSL certificate (free with most hosts) so your site loads on **https://** — AdSense and visitors both trust secure sites more.

## Step 3: Write Content That Ranks on Google

Your blog will not earn money without readers, and readers come from content that solves real problems. In Nepal, the topics that work best are education (exam notes, essays, study guides), Nepali recipes, travel, local news, technology, and personal finance.

Follow these rules for every article:

- Write at least **800–1,000 words** of original, useful content. Thin pages are the number one reason AdSense rejects Nepali blogs.
- Research the exact phrase students or readers type into Google, and use it naturally in your title, first paragraph, and headings.
- Break the article into clear headings so readers and Google can scan it easily.
- Add your own examples, photos, and experience — content that only you could write always beats copied content.
- Publish consistently. One article every 3–4 days for six months will do more for your blog than twenty articles in one week followed by silence.

## Step 4: Get Google AdSense Approval in Nepal

AdSense is the first monetization method most Nepali bloggers try, and it is the one that works automatically once approved. The review process in Nepal is strict because of past misuse, so follow this checklist carefully:

1. **Publish at least 20–30 quality articles** before applying. Do not apply with a five-post blog.
2. Create **About, Contact, Privacy Policy and Terms** pages and link them in your footer and menu.
3. Make sure every page loads fast on mobile — most Nepali readers use mobile data.
4. Do not copy content from other websites, and do not publish low-value scraped news or reworded AI spam. Originality is the entire game.
5. Apply with your **official domain** (not the free blogspot subdomain) for the best chances — though Blogger with a custom domain also works.
6. If you are rejected, read the exact reason in your AdSense account, fix it, and wait 2–4 weeks before reapplying. Applying again immediately makes things worse.

## Step 5: Other Ways to Earn Money From Your Blog

### Affiliate Marketing

Recommend products you genuinely use, using affiliate links (for example, Daraz, Amazon, hosting companies like Hostinger). When a reader buys through your link, you earn a commission. This has the highest earning potential after your blog grows.

### Sponsored Posts

Once your blog has traffic, Nepali companies, schools and local brands will pay for sponsored articles and reviews. A blog with 10,000 monthly visitors can earn NPR 3,000–10,000 per sponsored post.

### Selling Digital Products

Turn your knowledge into income — sell PDF study notes, e-books, templates or online courses to your readers. This is the most reliable income because you control it completely.

### Freelance Services

Use your blog as a portfolio to offer writing, editing, or website services. Many Nepali bloggers earn more from services than from ads.

## Realistic Expectations

A new blog in Nepal typically earns nothing for the first 3–6 months. With consistent publishing, many blogs start earning NPR 2,000–5,000 per month from AdSense by month 6–12, and more from affiliate marketing and sponsored posts after that. The blogs that earn six-figure monthly incomes are the ones that kept publishing for two to three years.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I start a blog in Nepal for free?

Yes. Blogger.com lets you start completely free, and WordPress.org only requires the cost of a domain and hosting. You can start for less than NPR 2,000.

### How long does it take to earn money from a blog in Nepal?

Most successful bloggers take 6–12 months of consistent work before seeing meaningful income. Blogs that promise income in days are lying.

### Do I need a custom domain for AdSense?

A custom domain (like yourname.com) gives you a much better chance of AdSense approval than a free blogspot subdomain. It also looks professional to readers.

### Which topics are best for a Nepali blog?

Education and exam materials, Nepali language content, travel, food, technology and personal finance are the topics with the most search demand in Nepal.

### What is the minimum content needed for AdSense approval?

There is no official number, but blogs with 20–30 original, 800+ word articles with proper About, Contact and Privacy pages have the best approval rate.
